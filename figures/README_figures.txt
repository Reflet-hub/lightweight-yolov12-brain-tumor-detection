Figures 1 and 4-7 of the revised manuscript

Data: unzip fig_data.zip in this folder (it creates ./data):
  - <config>/test<k>_seed42/{summary.json, test_curves.npz, test_confusion_matrix.npy, train/results.csv}
    for the configurations adopted, frozen and yolov12n and the test folds 1-5 (seed 42)
  - <config>/test1_seed42/test_eval/predictions.json (test predictions of rotation 1)
  - gt_fold1.json: ground-truth boxes of fold 1 (normalized xyxy; class 0 meningioma, 1 glioma, 2 pituitary tumor)
  - benchmark_images.json: the 100 fold-1 slices of the latency benchmark (see deployment/benchmark_images.csv)

Scripts (Python 3 with matplotlib, numpy and Pillow; font Liberation Sans), run from this folder:
  fig1.py      Fig. 1, study design
  figs456.py   Figs. 4, 5 and 6 (training curves, precision-recall and F1 curves, confusion matrices of
               rotation 1 at the fold-specific operating threshold); also writes fig6_info.json
  fig7.py      Fig. 7, detections on four fold-1 test slices (rotation-1 models, operating threshold);
               needs the PNG slices listed in deployment/benchmark_images.csv in a folder benchmark_images/
               (or a folder given by the environment variable BENCHMARK_IMAGES)
  cmlib.py     box matching used by Figs. 6 and 7 (Ultralytics rule: IoU > 0.45, highest IoU wins)
Figures 2 and 3 are not generated by these scripts.
